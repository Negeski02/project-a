The creator of all human connection

Unique

Habitat:

The cat’s cradle where all things are connected

Appearance:

The creature is made of shapes in most of the time only circles, rectangles and lines, that respectively represents a nucleus, arms and legs and antennae

Key Characteristics:

  • The main characteristics of my creature is that its made from the the mutation of a imaginary cat’s cradle, so as far as the cat’s cradle is being moved the creature is also mutating, aiming to represent that from new interactions of the cradle the creature is fully being created, but suddenly something happens, and the creature is fully impacted by it: a disintegration.

What we know so far about "LINES OF LINE" ? We know that “Lines of Life:” is a finding that show how exists a bigger Creator that controls all ways of living in its cat’s cradle, and each line of it, is a way of “Lines of Life” existence, even though when the creature is broken in pieces with a disintegration allowed by the cat’s cradle Creator, its strong nucleus make all the rest of the pieces come back together to life
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature The creature is already moving as the elastics on the cradle moves, so as any involuntary moves on the cradle, the creature keeps moving independently, if you HOLD and DRAG the creature will move around the canvas, if you MOVE IT far apart the center the creature will will disintegrate, as you CLICK on the center the creature will also change, also the elastics are going to move
